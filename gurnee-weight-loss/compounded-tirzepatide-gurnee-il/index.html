    Compounded tirzepatide is prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy for an individual patient rather than mass produced. The active ingredient matches the brand-name drugs.     Side Effects of Compounded Tirzepatide in Gurnee

    Nausea, diarrhea, constipation and vomiting are the most common side effects, and they usually ease within a few weeks. A dosing mistake can cause severe versions of these symptoms.

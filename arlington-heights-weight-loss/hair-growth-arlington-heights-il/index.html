    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Arlington Heights, IL

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Arlington Heights

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. It is driven by DHT, a hormone made from testosterone that slowly shrinks scalp follicles in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, which is why it follows a predictable pattern at the temples and crown. Finasteride lowers DHT, and minoxidil supports growth directly. Combined use tends to work better than one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Pattern loss is the usual reason, though low ferritin, thyroid trouble and postpartum shedding can mimic it. Minoxidil is the main FDA-approved option for women. Some women add off-label spironolactone or low-dose oral minoxidil.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Arlington Heights

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Topical minoxidil extends the growth phase and thickens individual hairs. The pairing is the standard. Some men use a compounded topical spray that combines both, which is not FDA-approved but may reduce whole-body exposure to finasteride.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. It is FDA-approved for an enlarged prostate and used off-label for hair loss, most often in men who did not get enough from finasteride after a year. It lingers. Its half-life is about five weeks, so screening and side effect planning take more care. Minoxidil rounds it out.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Arlington Heights

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. A compounded minoxidil solution with GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, is one option, with minoxidil doing the main work and GHK-Cu adding early, mostly lab-based support for scalp and follicle health. It is compounded. The blend has no FDA approval. It suits women, and men avoiding finasteride.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. They also ask about pregnancy plans, worries about sexual side effects, past depression and whether you would rather take a pill or apply something to your scalp twice a day. Most men begin with the finasteride and minoxidil pairing. Minoxidil is the usual first step for women.

    What to Expect Over Time in Arlington Heights

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. That is why most treatments take three to six months to show a visible change and around twelve months to reach their full effect, even when they are working from the first week. Some people shed more in weeks two through eight of minoxidil, which usually means follicles are cycling into a new growth phase. Results last only as long as treatment does.
